Lab Meetings

Each week the GATAS Lab holds a lab meeting where one or two lab members give a 30-60 minute presentation. We use these presentations for practice talks or for educating each other on open problems. Below is the running schedule of those meetings.

Slides are linked when the speaker posted a deck to the AlgebraicJulia Presentations repository. Plenty of meetings are working sessions with nothing to post, and those rows show a dash instead. Weeks cancelled for a conference, a holiday, or a term break are not listed.
