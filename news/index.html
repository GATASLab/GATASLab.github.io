Summer 2026

Four lab members spend the summer at national labs and Georgia Tech

Andersen Wall at AFRL, Joana Bou Barcelo and Liam Kunzendorf at Georgia Tech, and George Rauta at ARL Adelphi.

May 2026

Tyler Hanks and Luke Morris receive their PhDs

The lab’s first two PhD graduates, both hooded in May 2026.

27 April 2026

A Brazilian category theory hub, from Ben Bumpus's group

Categorias Brasil — a repository for all things category theory in Brasil, built by Ben Bumpus’s student Paulo Vitor Macedo Dias.

15 April 2026

George Rauta presents Decapodes CFD at WCCM–ECCOMAS

Low-Mach compressible Navier–Stokes on rectilinear grids, via discrete exterior calculus, in Munich.

February 2026

Ben Bumpus is a professor at the University of São Paulo

A professorship at IME at the University of São Paulo, where he now leads the CUÍCA lab.

January 2026

Wilmer Leal promoted to research scientist

Now a research scientist in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, with the Nonlinear Controls and Robotics group.

19 November 2025

James Fairbanks named an ASEMFL Rising Star

The Academy of Science, Engineering and Medicine of Florida recognized James Fairbanks at its 2025 Annual Meeting.

October 2024

A minisymposium at SIAM Mathematics of Data Science

James Fairbanks organized a minisymposium at MDS24 in Atlanta, with Tyler Hanks, Evan Patterson and Henry Adams speaking.

2022

Kris Brown joins the Topos Institute

A research scientist at Topos since 2022, and still a maintainer of the AlgebraicJulia libraries.
